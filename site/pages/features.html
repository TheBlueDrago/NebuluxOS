Features

Everything in Nebulux OS

A complete desktop that starts from a USB stick, with its own apps, real hardware support, and updates that come to you.

The desktop

{{icon:apps}}

Start

Pinned apps, All apps from A to Z, and Recommended with what you opened lately. Search finds apps, Settings and files.

{{icon:pin}}

The taskbar

Pin the apps you use. Widgets on the left, the Search box, Task View, and the clock and quick settings on the right.

{{icon:window}}

Windows

Drag, snap to the top or sides, resize from any edge, minimize, maximize and close. Snap layouts for halves, thirds and quarters.

{{icon:spark}}

Task View and Widgets

All your open windows at a glance with the Nebulux key and Tab. Weather where you are with the Nebulux key and W.

{{icon:key}}

Shortcuts

Nebulux opens Start. Add D for the desktop, E for files, L to lock, the arrows to snap. Alt and Tab switch windows.

{{icon:user}}

Sign-in

A lock screen with the clock, several accounts with passwords, and a Guest start on USBs that don't save changes.

Apps

{{icon:apps}}

The Nebulux Store

Real apps like Steam, Minecraft, Spotify, Discord, Zoom, Slack, VLC, OBS Studio, GIMP, Krita, Blender, LibreOffice, Firefox and Visual Studio Code. About 70 more as web apps.

{{icon:globe}}

The web

A full web browser with tabs for Google, YouTube, Gmail and every website. Sign in with Google once, and it's ready.

{{icon:folder}}

File Explorer

Your folders, the Recycle Bin and USB drives. Cut, copy, paste, rename, search, and Icons or Details view.

{{icon:play}}

Photos and Media Player

Your pictures and screenshots with zoom and rotate. Music and videos in MP4, WebM, MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A and FLAC.

{{icon:spark}}

Notepad, Paint, Snipping Tool

Write and save text files, draw, and take screenshots, with a timer if you like.

{{icon:window}}

Task Manager

See what's running and close anything that's stuck.

Getting things done

{{icon:window}}

Virtual desktops

Separate desktops for school, work and games. Add, rename and drag windows between them in Task View.

{{icon:apps}}

Clipboard history

Nebulux + V: the last 25 things you copied, from any app. Pin the ones you use often.

{{icon:folder}}

Tabs in File Explorer

Several folders in one window. Ctrl + T, Ctrl + W and Ctrl + Tab.

{{icon:pin}}

Sticky Notes

Colored notes that save as you type, with search and Undo.

{{icon:spark}}

Focus sessions

Do not disturb for 15 to 60 minutes from the Clock app.

{{icon:play}}

Get Started

A quick tour with every shortcut, for anyone new to Nebulux OS.

Your computer

{{icon:wifi}}

Wi-Fi

Drivers for the newest Wi-Fi 6 and 6E chips. Connect, forget saved networks, join hidden ones, and a troubleshooter that fixes problems for you.

{{icon:bt}}

Bluetooth

Add headphones, mice and keyboards, pair them, and remove them, from Settings or quick settings.

{{icon:tv}}

Screens and sound

A TV or monitor on HDMI: duplicate, second screen only, or this screen only. Pick which speakers play sound, or cast to a wireless TV.

{{icon:battery}}

Battery and Battery saver

A battery bar that goes down with the charge and changes color, and Battery saver that turns it yellow and makes the battery last.

{{icon:mouse}}

Mouse, night light and storage

Pointer speed from Slower to Faster, night light on a schedule, and how much space is left on your USB.

{{icon:refresh}}

Updates

Nebulux OS finds its own updates and asks: Later, Remind me or Never. Each one is checked before it's used.
