Privacy

Last updated November 22, 2026. Nebulux OS and this website are owned and operated by Nebulux LLC.

The short version

Nebulux OS keeps your accounts, passwords, files and settings on your USB stick. It doesn't send anything about you to Nebulux. This website has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Nebulux OS

  • Accounts and passwords stay on your USB. Passwords are kept only as a salted, scrambled form.
  • Update checks download a small file listing the newest version. Like any download, this shares your computer's internet address with the server; nothing else is sent.
  • Time zone: when Set time zone automatically is on (Settings > Time & language), Nebulux OS asks a time zone service (ipapi.co, or worldtimeapi.org) once each start which time zone your internet address is in. Turn it off to pick the time zone yourself.
  • Weather in Widgets sends only the city you type to Open-Meteo, a free weather service. You can turn it off in Settings > Privacy & security.
  • Apps, websites and Google sign-in you use in Nebulux OS follow their own privacy policies.

This website

The site is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard technical logs (such as internet addresses and pages requested) to run and protect the service. We don't use cookies for tracking or advertising. "Try it in your browser" saves its pretend accounts and settings in your own browser only.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect Nebulux OS and this website, such as checking every update against its fingerprint before it's used, but no system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee it.

Children

Nebulux OS doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
