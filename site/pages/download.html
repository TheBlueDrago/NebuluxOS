1. Download

Press Download above. The file is about {{size}}, so it can take a few minutes.

To check the download isn't damaged (optional), open PowerShell in your Downloads folder and run Get-FileHash .\{{file}}. The number should match the SHA-256 above.

2. Put it on your USB

Writing Nebulux OS to a USB erases everything on that USB. Copy your files off it first.
  1. Get Rufus from its official site, rufus.ie. It's free.
  2. Plug in your USB and open Rufus. Choose:
    • Device: your USB stick. Check its name and size: whatever you pick is erased.
    • Boot selection: press SELECT and pick {{file}}.
    • Persistent partition size: about 4 GB or more. This makes Nebulux OS remember your accounts, settings, Wi-Fi passwords and files. At 0, it starts fresh every time and offers a Guest start.
    • Partition scheme: GPT for most computers from 2013 on, MBR for older ones.
  3. Press START. If it asks about ISOHybrid, choose Write in ISO Image mode (Recommended). If it asks to download extra files, say Yes.
  4. When it says READY, close Rufus and safely remove the USB.

3. Start your computer from the USB

  1. Plug in the USB and turn the computer on, or restart it.
  2. Right away, tap the boot menu key until a list of drives appears:

Or, from the system that's on the computer now: open Power, hold Shift and choose Restart, then Use a device and your USB.

  1. Pick your USB from the list (it may say "UEFI" and the USB's brand).
  2. Nebulux OS starts by itself: the Nebulux logo, then first-time setup. Make your account, pick your region, keyboard, Wi-Fi and look, and you're on the desktop.
To go back to your usual system, use Start > Power > Shut down, take the USB out, and turn the computer on.

If it doesn't start

  • It goes straight to your usual system. The boot menu key wasn't pressed in time. Restart and tap it as soon as the screen turns on, or use the Shift and Restart way above.
  • "Secure Boot violation". Nebulux OS supports Secure Boot, but some computers still refuse. Turn Secure Boot off in the computer's setup screen (usually F2, Del or F10 at power-on), and back on afterwards if you like.
  • A black screen for more than 2 minutes. Restart once more. Still stuck? Note the computer's brand and model and see Help.
  • Mac computers. Hold Option at power-on and choose "EFI Boot". Macs with Apple chips (M1 and newer) can't start other systems from USB.
