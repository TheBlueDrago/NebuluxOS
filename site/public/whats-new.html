What's new

New in Nebulux OS 3.2

The same list Nebulux OS shows you after it updates. On Nebulux OS 3.1? Write the new 3.2 file to your USB once: it has a newer Linux kernel with drivers for the newest Wi-Fi chips. After that, updates come by themselves.

3.2Latest version

Wi-Fi on newer laptops

Nebulux OS now runs on the newer Linux kernel 6.12, with drivers for Wi-Fi 6 and 6E chips that had none before: Realtek RTL8852BE, RTL8852AE, RTL8852CE and RTL8922AE (in many Lenovo, HP and ASUS laptops), MediaTek MT7921 and MT7922, and the newest Intel chips. Their Bluetooth works too.

Wi-Fi that stays connected

Wi-Fi power saving and changing addresses are off, which kept Realtek and MediaTek chips dropping the connection. Lenovo IdeaPads whose Wi-Fi said it was switched off are fixed by themselves.

Network troubleshooter

Settings > Network & internet > Troubleshoot checks the Wi-Fi chip, its driver, the Wi-Fi switch, the connection and the internet, says what is wrong in plain words, and Try to fix restarts the Wi-Fi driver and the network for you. It can also save a report to Documents.

School and work Wi-Fi

Networks that need a username as well as a password (WPA2 and WPA3-Enterprise, like most school and office Wi-Fi) connect from Settings > Network & internet.

Mobile hotspot

Settings > Network & internet > Mobile hotspot shares this computer's internet with your phone or another computer over Wi-Fi.

Known and hidden networks

Forget a saved network that keeps failing, join a hidden network by its name, press Show to see the password while you type it, and Properties shows the connected network's signal, band, speed and IP address.

Virtual desktops

Task View (Nebulux + Tab) has your desktops at the bottom: add one, rename it, drag windows between them. Ctrl + Nebulux + Left or Right switches, Ctrl + Nebulux + D adds one, Ctrl + Nebulux + F4 closes it.

Clipboard history

Nebulux + V shows the last 25 things you copied, in Nebulux OS and in real apps. Click one to paste it, pin the ones you need often. Turn it off in Settings > System.

File Explorer tabs and Quick access

Ctrl + T opens a tab, Ctrl + W closes it, and a middle-click or Open in new tab opens a folder in its own tab. Right-click a folder and Pin to Quick access to keep it in the sidebar.

Sticky Notes

Quick colored notes that save as you type, with search and Undo. Your notes show in Widgets too.

Focus sessions and alarms

Clock > Focus: 15 to 60 minutes with Do not disturb on until it is done. Clock > Alarms: wake-up and reminder alarms, once or on the days you pick. Both keep going with Clock closed.

Calendar events

Click the clock on the taskbar, pick a day and add an event, with a time if you like. Days with events get a dot, and you get a reminder when one starts.

Your laptop's keys

The volume, mute, microphone and brightness keys work, with a small indicator above the taskbar. Nebulux + 1 to 9 opens the apps on the taskbar, and Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad turns tap to click and natural scrolling on or off.

The right time

Nebulux OS sets its time zone by itself (or pick it in Settings > Time & language), and keeps the computer's clock the way Windows does, so going back to Windows never shows the wrong time.

Sleep like a laptop

The power button and closing the lid put the computer to sleep, and it asks for your password when it wakes up. Settings > System > Power can make the power button shut down instead.

Night light on a schedule

Settings > System: night light comes on and goes off by itself at the hours you pick.

Storage

Settings > System > Storage shows how much space is left on your USB, what uses it, and empties the Recycle Bin.

Startup apps and keyboard

Settings > Apps > Startup apps: pick apps that open by themselves when you sign in. Settings > Time & language changes the keyboard layout any time.

Get Started

A quick tour of Nebulux OS with every shortcut. Open it from Start.

Battery time left

The battery says how long it will last, or how long until it is full, in quick settings, Settings and Widgets.

Your own background and picture

Settings > Personalization > Browse photos makes any picture your background, and Settings > Accounts gives your account a picture for sign-in, Start and Settings.

Photos, Calculator, Notepad and more

Notepad has Find and Replace (Ctrl + F, Ctrl + H), Snipping Tool crops a screenshot to the part you want, Photos plays a slideshow, Calculator keeps a history of answers (and Ctrl + C copies one), color filters (grayscale or inverted) are in Accessibility, the far right corner of the taskbar shows the desktop, and hovering an open app on the taskbar shows a preview of its window.

A smarter lock screen and Widgets

The lock screen shows Wi-Fi and the battery. Widgets show your Sticky Notes and the battery.

Earlier updates

Updates by themselves

Nebulux OS now finds its own updates. Install them tonight (Later), get a reminder in 30 minutes, or skip one (Never) and install it any time from Settings > Nebulux Update.

Real apps in the Nebulux Store

Steam, Minecraft, Spotify, Discord, Zoom, Slack, Telegram, VLC, OBS Studio, GIMP, Krita, Blender, LibreOffice, Firefox, Google Chrome, Visual Studio Code, Signal and Audacity install as real apps. Netflix, WhatsApp, Office and about 60 more open as web apps in their own windows.

Window buttons for every app

Real apps and app windows have minimize, maximize and close. Minimized apps come back from the taskbar.

Pin apps your way

Search in Start and use the details on the right: Open, Pin to taskbar, Show on or Hide from desktop, Uninstall. Or right-click an app on the desktop.

A USB that doesn't save changes

Start right away as Guest instead of setting up every time.

Better Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

Wi-Fi turns on even when the laptop had it blocked, Bluetooth devices pair more easily, and both say exactly what chip the computer has when something can't work. Opening Google or YouTube without internet takes you to the Wi-Fi list.

A battery that shows how much is left

The battery icon's bar goes down as the battery drains: green while charging, orange at 20%, red at 10%. Battery saver (in quick settings and Settings > System) turns it yellow and turns off effects so the battery lasts longer, and it turns on by itself at 20%.

A better mouse

The standard pointer speed by default, with Slower and Faster in Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Mouse. The Nebulux Store's rows have arrow buttons, so you never have to aim for a thin scroll bar.

Settings like you know them

Home, System, Bluetooth & devices, Network & internet, Personalization, Apps, Accounts, Time & language, Accessibility, Privacy & security and Nebulux Update.

A real web browser

Google, YouTube and websites open in a full browser with tabs, Google sign-in, Gmail and YouTube, right above the taskbar.

Your files

File Explorer with your folders, the Recycle Bin and USB drives, plus Photos, Media Player and Snipping Tool.

Screens and TV

Plug in a TV with HDMI to show Nebulux OS on it, pick where sound plays, or cast to a wireless TV.

Shortcuts with the Nebulux key

Nebulux opens Start; Nebulux + Tab, D, E, L, W and the arrow keys do the rest, even over websites. Alt + Tab switches windows.

First-time setup

Make your account, then pick your region, keyboard, Wi-Fi, privacy and look, and sign in with Google.
