What's new

New in Nebulux OS 4.0

The same list Nebulux OS shows you after it updates. On Nebulux OS 3.1? Write the new 4.0 file to your USB once: it has a newer Linux kernel with drivers for the newest Wi-Fi chips. After that, updates come by themselves.

4.0Latest version

A whole new look

Glass that takes a little color from your background, a taskbar that floats above the bottom of the screen, stars that slowly twinkle over your wallpaper, and springier menus and windows.

Themes

Settings > Personalization > Themes sets your background, dark or light mode, accent color, corners and glass in one click: Nebulux, Aurora, Ember, Polar, Pearl or Daybreak.

Corners and glass your way

Pick square, round or extra round corners, and clear, frosted or solid glass, in Settings > Personalization.

Window buttons

Keep the plain window buttons, or switch to three colored dots in Settings > Personalization.

Live Task View and Alt + Tab

Task View and Alt + Tab show a live picture of each window instead of just its icon.

An accent color that matches

Choose Auto under Accent color and Nebulux OS picks a color from your background.

Seven new backgrounds

Silk Waves, Star Dunes, Polar Lights, Morning Haze, Nebulux Four, Aurora Ribbon and Pearl Dawn.

Pick your lock screen picture

Settings > Personalization > Lock screen picture shows any background on the lock screen, or the same one as your desktop.

A brighter Settings

Every setting has a small colored icon, and the menu marker slides to the page you pick.

Notifications and calendar as cards

The clock opens your notifications and the calendar as two floating cards.

Widgets, better

Widgets shows what's next in your calendar and your reminders, with a color for each card.

Folders you can spot

Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music and Videos each have their own icon, and Start has one-click buttons for them beside the power button.

Pick your look when you set up

The last step of setup shows the themes, so a new account looks the way you like from the start.

Pointer color

Settings > Accessibility > Pointer color makes the pointer white, black or your accent color.

Sounds

A soft chime when you sign in, and, if you want it, a gentle sound for notifications. Both are in Settings > Sound.

Snap into thirds

On wide screens, the snap layouts under the maximize button add three columns, and a wide middle with narrow sides.

Music you can see

Media Player shows a glow and dancing bars while a song plays. Voice Recorder recordings play like songs too.

An analog clock

The Clock app shows a clock face with hands beside the time.

Paint, tidier

Paint's tools are icons in one row: Brush, Line, Box, Circle, Fill and Eraser, then Undo and Clear.

Reminders that count

Reminders' taskbar button shows how many reminders are due.

Task Manager graphs

Processor and memory graphs with a grid and a glowing line.

A better update window

Updates now show what's in them as cards, with Update now and a live progress bar, plus Later, Remind me and Never.

Screen savers

Settings > Personalization > Screen saver: a Starfield or a Floating clock after the computer sits still, and it can lock when it ends.

The snap bar

Drag a window to the top of the screen and drop it on a layout to snap it there.

Stacks

Right-click the desktop and pick Use stacks to pile your desktop files by kind: Pictures, Documents, Music and more.

Edit quick settings

Press the pencil in quick settings to hide or show tiles. New tiles: Screenshot and Large pointer.

Jump lists

Right-click Notepad, Photos, Media Player or File Explorer on the taskbar to open a recent file.

Peek and Shake

Rest the pointer on the far right of the taskbar to see the desktop through your windows. Shake a window by its title bar to minimize the others.

Bouncy taskbar

An app's taskbar button gives a little bounce when it opens.

A living Weather app

The weather now sits on an animated sky: sunshine, drifting clouds, rain, snow, storms, fog, and stars at night.

Preview pane

Press Preview in File Explorer to see the selected file on the right: a big picture or the first lines of text, with its size and date.

Focus from the clock

The calendar flyout has Focus: pick the minutes and start. Do not disturb stays on until it ends.

Ctrl + Alt + Delete

Opens Lock, Switch user, Sign out, Change a password and Task Manager, with Restart and Shut down in the corner.

Icon sizes

Right-click the desktop and pick Large, Medium or Small icons under View.

Gallery

File Explorer has Gallery: every picture from Pictures, Desktop and Downloads in one place, newest first.

App names on the taskbar

Settings > Personalization > Taskbar can show each open app's name next to its icon.

Music on the lock screen

When something is playing, the lock screen shows it with play, pause and next.

A background for each desktop

Right-click a desktop in Task View to give it its own background.

Notifications with icons

Each notification shows a fitting icon, and you can dismiss them one at a time.

Shooting stars

Now and then a shooting star crosses the living background.

Picture thumbnails in Start

Pictures you opened lately show a small picture in Recommended.

Back up to a USB drive

Settings > System > Back up copies your Desktop, Documents, Pictures, Music and Videos to a USB drive in one click.

Taskbar items

Show search as a box, just the icon, or not at all, and turn the Task view and Widgets buttons on or off.

Night light strength

Settings > System > Display has a slider for how warm Night light makes the screen.

Windows that grow from your click

A new window grows out of the taskbar button, Start tile or desktop icon you clicked.

Choose a fit

For your own background picture: Fill, Fit, Stretch or Center.

Snap Assist

Snap a window to one half and your other windows show up in the other half, with a live picture of each. Pick one to fill it.

Corner snapping

Drag a window into a corner of the screen to give it a quarter.

Your day on the lock screen

While the computer is locked, the lock screen shows your next event and how many reminders are due. Settings > Personalization > Lock screen details turns it off.

Themes on Settings Home

Settings Home shows the six themes under Personalize your device.

Recycle Bin on the desktop

The Recycle Bin sits on your desktop and fills up when something is in it. Right-click it to empty it.

Select icons with a box

Drag on an empty part of the desktop to draw a box around icons.

Toasts you can close

Click a pop-up message to close it. A thin bar shows how long it stays.

A new start-up screen

A soft glow breathes behind the Nebulux logo while lights pulse under it as your computer starts.

Quick Search

Press Nebulux + Space for one search box in the middle of the screen: apps, settings, your files, apps from the Store, sums like 12*12, conversions like 5 km in miles, and the weather.

Stage

Settings > Personalization > Stage keeps only the window you're using out. Your other windows wait as cards on the left, one click away.

Desktop widgets

Put a clock, the weather, what's next in your calendar and the battery right on your wallpaper.

Calendar and Reminders

Two new apps: Calendar shows the whole month with colored events, and Reminders keeps your to-do list and rings when something is due.

Peek

Select a file in File Explorer and press Space to see it right away: pictures, videos, music, text and PDFs. Press Space again to close it.

App Grid

Nebulux + G shows every app, big, on the whole screen. Type to find one, or use the arrow keys.

Hot corners

Push the pointer into a corner of the screen to show the desktop, open Task View, the App Grid, Quick Search, Start, a new sticky note, or lock.

Screenshots, better

Nebulux + Shift + S lets you drag over just the part of the screen you want. After every screenshot, a small picture of it floats in the corner: click it to open it.

File Explorer, better

Right-click a file to tag it Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue or Purple, or to see its Properties. The sidebar has Recent, with the files you opened lately, and your tags.

Versions in Notepad

Notepad keeps earlier versions of your notes as you write. Press Versions to go back to one.

Saved cities in Weather

Press the star to save a city, then switch between your cities with one click.

Photos, better

Crop a picture in Edit, press the heart to make it a favorite, and Favorites shows only those.

Picture in picture

Videos in Media Player can keep playing in a small window on top while you do other things.

Now playing

While Media Player plays, quick settings shows what's playing, with play, pause and next.

Screen time

Settings > System shows how long you used each app today. It's counted only on this computer.

A background that follows the day

Morning, midday, sunset and night backgrounds that change by themselves, and a new background made for 4.0: Nebulux Four.

Magnifier

Nebulux + Plus zooms in around the pointer, Nebulux + Minus zooms out, and Nebulux + Esc stops.

Increase contrast

Settings > Accessibility > Increase contrast gives stronger outlines and brighter text.

A taskbar your way

Hide the taskbar until you need it, let its icons grow under the pointer, and show the battery percentage or seconds in the clock.

Shake to find the pointer

Lost the pointer? Shake the mouse and it grows for a moment.

Smoother windows

Windows glide into their taskbar button when you minimize them, and back out when you open them.

Alt + F4

Closes the window you're using, real apps too.

Caps Lock warning

Password boxes tell you when Caps Lock is on.

Earlier updates

Sticky notes on your desktop

Press the pin on a sticky note to put it on your desktop. Type in it, drag it by the top, and take it off with its x.

Star Tiles

A new sliding puzzle game in the Nebulux Store: put the tiles in order from 1 to 15. Works without the internet, and keeps your best.

Countdowns

Clock > Countdown counts the days to a birthday, a trip or the last day of school.

Find your apps

Settings > Apps has a search box.

Tabs in Notepad

Keep several notes and text files open in one Notepad. Each tab is named from its first line, and your tabs come back next time.

Edit your photos

Photos > Edit changes brightness, contrast and color, adds a look like Black and white, Warm or Vintage, rotates, and saves a copy.

Shapes and Fill in Paint

Draw lines, boxes and circles, and pour color into any shape with Fill. The canvas now fills the whole window.

A scientific calculator

Calculator > Scientific does sin, cos, tan, log, ln, powers, roots, factorials, pi and brackets. Type a whole sum or tap it in.

Snooze

Alarms and timers ring with a card: Snooze for 5 minutes or Dismiss. They keep beeping until you answer, for up to a minute.

Quicker backgrounds

Right-click the desktop and pick Next background.

Keep a window on top

Right-click a window's title bar for Minimize, Maximize, Keep on top and Close. A window kept on top stays above the others.

Sort your files

File Explorer sorts by name, date, type or size: click a column in Details view, or use Sort.

Notepad, a little better

Turn Word wrap on or off, and see the line and column you're on.

Close apps from the taskbar

Right-click Netflix, Spotify or any other app on the taskbar and pick Close window.

Join the hotspot with a QR code

When Mobile hotspot is on, Settings shows a QR code. Point your phone's camera at it to join without typing the password.

Clean up your USB

Settings > System > Storage can clean up temporary files (website and app caches, and app parts nothing uses) and says how much space it freed. Your files, sign-ins and settings stay.

Draw on screenshots

Snipping Tool has Draw: mark up a screenshot with four pen colors and Undo, then save it as a copy.

A large mouse pointer

Settings > Accessibility > Large mouse pointer gives you a bigger arrow, hand and text cursor that are easier to see.

Auto mode

Settings > Personalization > Choose your mode has Auto: light windows and menus from 7 AM, dark from 7 PM.

Weather on the lock screen

The lock screen shows the weather under the date.

World clocks

The Clock app shows the time in other cities, like London or Tokyo, and how far ahead or behind they are.

A unit converter

Calculator > Converter changes length, weight, temperature, volume, speed and time.

Your photo as your background

Photos has Set as background for the picture you're looking at, like one from the Camera.

Shuffle and Repeat

Media Player can shuffle your music and videos, and repeat them all or just one.

Arrange your taskbar

Drag a pinned app sideways on the taskbar to put it where you want.

Camera

Take photos and videos with your computer's camera. Photos go to Pictures and videos to Videos, with a self-timer of 3 or 10 seconds.

Voice Recorder

Record your voice, a lesson or a meeting, and see it move as you talk. Recordings are saved in Music, ready to play.

Weather

A full Weather app: the weather now, the next 24 hours and the whole week, for any city or wherever you are.

Every shortcut in one place

Press Nebulux + / (or open Get Started > Shortcuts) to see every keyboard shortcut.

A new background every day

Settings > Personalization can give you a new background every day.

Weather where you are

Widgets can use your location for the weather, instead of typing your city.

A more colorful Store

Every app's page in the Nebulux Store glows in that app's own color.

A faster screen

On computers where Nebulux OS draws the screen without the graphics chip, windows, menus and the taskbar use solid glass instead of blurring what's behind them, so everything moves faster.

You see apps opening

When you open YouTube, Netflix, Steam or any other app, its logo and Opening show in the middle of the screen until its window is up, so a click never looks like it did nothing.

Google's shortcuts with their logos

Gmail, YouTube, Maps, Drive, News and Translate under Google's search box show their own logos.

Small touches

Settings > Personalization shows a tiny window and taskbar in your colors, notifications step aside instead of covering quick settings and the calendar, and setup's text boxes have one clean glow.

Apps open fast

YouTube, Poki, Xbox Cloud Gaming and every other app now use the same safe graphics as Nebulux OS, so they load right away instead of hanging on a blank page, and they keep their cache in memory instead of writing it to the USB stick.

Xbox Cloud Gaming and GeForce NOW open right away

They no longer wait for the movie player to install first.

Movie apps don't make you wait

Netflix, Disney+ and the other movie apps open at once while their movie player sets itself up in the background.

A sharper look

Rounder windows and menus with a light edge, a soft glow behind the window you're using, a taskbar with a sheen, and smoother hover effects.

Apps open as their own apps

Poki, Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Spotify and every other app from the Nebulux Store open in their own window with their own taskbar button, not in a browser tab. Games and videos get the graphics card, so pages no longer say the browser is unsupported.

Netflix, Disney+ and other movie apps play

Apps with protected movies and music set up their video player by themselves the first time you open one (it takes a minute, once).

Google as its own app

Google opens as the real Google app in its own window, with your searches, sign-in and Gmail, instead of a web page inside another app.

Real app logos

Netflix, Hulu, Spotify, Discord, Docs, Sheets, Word, Excel and every other app that isn't part of Nebulux OS shows its own logo. New icons for File Explorer, the Nebulux Store and Settings.

A hotspot your phone joins

Mobile hotspot now uses standard WPA2 security on 2.4 GHz, so phones accept the right password instead of saying it is wrong.

Wi-Fi on newer laptops

Nebulux OS now runs on the newer Linux kernel 6.12, with drivers for Wi-Fi 6 and 6E chips that had none before: Realtek RTL8852BE, RTL8852AE, RTL8852CE and RTL8922AE (in many Lenovo, HP and ASUS laptops), MediaTek MT7921 and MT7922, and the newest Intel chips. Their Bluetooth works too.

Wi-Fi that stays connected

Wi-Fi power saving and changing addresses are off, which kept Realtek and MediaTek chips dropping the connection. Lenovo IdeaPads whose Wi-Fi said it was switched off are fixed by themselves.

Network troubleshooter

Settings > Network & internet > Troubleshoot checks the Wi-Fi chip, its driver, the Wi-Fi switch, the connection and the internet, says what is wrong in plain words, and Try to fix restarts the Wi-Fi driver and the network for you. It can also save a report to Documents.

School and work Wi-Fi

Networks that need a username as well as a password (WPA2 and WPA3-Enterprise, like most school and office Wi-Fi) connect from Settings > Network & internet.

Mobile hotspot

Settings > Network & internet > Mobile hotspot shares this computer's internet with your phone or another computer over Wi-Fi.

Known and hidden networks

Forget a saved network that keeps failing, join a hidden network by its name, press Show to see the password while you type it, and Properties shows the connected network's signal, band, speed and IP address.

Virtual desktops

Task View (Nebulux + Tab) has your desktops at the bottom: add one, rename it, drag windows between them. Ctrl + Nebulux + Left or Right switches, Ctrl + Nebulux + D adds one, Ctrl + Nebulux + F4 closes it.

Clipboard history

Nebulux + V shows the last 25 things you copied, in Nebulux OS and in real apps. Click one to paste it, pin the ones you need often. Turn it off in Settings > System.

File Explorer tabs and Quick access

Ctrl + T opens a tab, Ctrl + W closes it, and a middle-click or Open in new tab opens a folder in its own tab. Right-click a folder and Pin to Quick access to keep it in the sidebar.

Sticky Notes

Quick colored notes that save as you type, with search and Undo. Your notes show in Widgets too.

Focus sessions and alarms

Clock > Focus: 15 to 60 minutes with Do not disturb on until it is done. Clock > Alarms: wake-up and reminder alarms, once or on the days you pick. Both keep going with Clock closed.

Calendar events

Click the clock on the taskbar, pick a day and add an event, with a time if you like. Days with events get a dot, and you get a reminder when one starts.

Your laptop's keys

The volume, mute, microphone and brightness keys work, with a small indicator above the taskbar. Nebulux + 1 to 9 opens the apps on the taskbar, and Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad turns tap to click and natural scrolling on or off.

Display scale

Settings > System > Display > Scale makes everything bigger (125% to 200%) on sharp laptop screens, web pages included.

The right time

Nebulux OS sets its time zone by itself (or pick it in Settings > Time & language), and keeps the computer's clock the way Windows does, so going back to Windows never shows the wrong time.

Sleep like a laptop

The power button and closing the lid put the computer to sleep, and it asks for your password when it wakes up. Settings > System > Power can make the power button shut down instead.

Night light on a schedule

Settings > System: night light comes on and goes off by itself at the hours you pick.

Storage

Settings > System > Storage shows how much space is left on your USB, what uses it, and empties the Recycle Bin.

Startup apps and keyboard

Settings > Apps > Startup apps: pick apps that open by themselves when you sign in. Settings > Time & language changes the keyboard layout any time.

Get Started

A quick tour of Nebulux OS with every shortcut. Open it from Start.

Battery time left

The battery says how long it will last, or how long until it is full, in quick settings, Settings and Widgets.

Your own background and picture

Settings > Personalization > Browse photos makes any picture your background, and Settings > Accounts gives your account a picture for sign-in, Start and Settings.

Photos, Calculator, Notepad and more

Notepad has Find and Replace (Ctrl + F, Ctrl + H), Snipping Tool crops a screenshot to the part you want, Photos plays a slideshow, Calculator keeps a history of answers (and Ctrl + C copies one), color filters (grayscale or inverted) are in Accessibility, the far right corner of the taskbar shows the desktop, and hovering an open app on the taskbar shows a preview of its window.

A smarter lock screen and Widgets

The lock screen shows Wi-Fi and the battery. Widgets show your Sticky Notes and the battery.

Updates by themselves

Nebulux OS now finds its own updates. Install them tonight (Later), get a reminder in 30 minutes, or skip one (Never) and install it any time from Settings > Nebulux Update.

Real apps in the Nebulux Store

Steam, Minecraft, Spotify, Discord, Zoom, Slack, Telegram, VLC, OBS Studio, GIMP, Krita, Blender, LibreOffice, Firefox, Google Chrome, Visual Studio Code, Signal and Audacity install as real apps. Netflix, WhatsApp, Office and about 60 more open as web apps in their own windows.

Window buttons for every app

Real apps and app windows have minimize, maximize and close. Minimized apps come back from the taskbar.

Pin apps your way

Search in Start and use the details on the right: Open, Pin to taskbar, Show on or Hide from desktop, Uninstall. Or right-click an app on the desktop.

A USB that doesn't save changes

Start right away as Guest instead of setting up every time.

Better Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

Wi-Fi turns on even when the laptop had it blocked, Bluetooth devices pair more easily, and both say exactly what chip the computer has when something can't work. Opening Google or YouTube without internet takes you to the Wi-Fi list.

A battery that shows how much is left

The battery icon's bar goes down as the battery drains: green while charging, orange at 20%, red at 10%. Battery saver (in quick settings and Settings > System) turns it yellow and turns off effects so the battery lasts longer, and it turns on by itself at 20%.

A better mouse

The standard pointer speed by default, with Slower and Faster in Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Mouse. The Nebulux Store's rows have arrow buttons, so you never have to aim for a thin scroll bar.

Settings like you know them

Home, System, Bluetooth & devices, Network & internet, Personalization, Apps, Accounts, Time & language, Accessibility, Privacy & security and Nebulux Update.

A real web browser

Google, YouTube and websites open in a full browser with tabs, Google sign-in, Gmail and YouTube, right above the taskbar.

Your files

File Explorer with your folders, the Recycle Bin and USB drives, plus Photos, Media Player and Snipping Tool.

Screens and TV

Plug in a TV with HDMI to show Nebulux OS on it, pick where sound plays, or cast to a wireless TV.

Shortcuts with the Nebulux key

Nebulux opens Start; Nebulux + Tab, D, E, L, W and the arrow keys do the rest, even over websites. Alt + Tab switches windows.

First-time setup

Make your account, then pick your region, keyboard, Wi-Fi, privacy and look, and sign in with Google.
