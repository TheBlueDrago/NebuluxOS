What's new

New in Nebulux OS 4.4

The same list Nebulux OS shows you after it updates. On Nebulux OS 3.1? Write the new 4.4 file to your USB once: it has a newer Linux kernel with drivers for the newest Wi-Fi chips. After that, updates come by themselves.

4.4Latest version

Sign in with a PIN

Settings > Accounts can set up a PIN of 4 to 12 numbers, a quicker way to sign in like Windows Hello. Your password still works, and the PIN is kept only as a scrambled code.

Nebulux Horizon

A new background that follows the time of day: the glowing edge of a planet under a violet nebula, at sunrise, in daylight, at dusk and at night. New accounts start with Horizon Dynamic; each time of day is also its own background in Settings > Personalization.

New icons

Settings has a glossy gear with a glowing nebula in the middle, Task Manager a glowing live chart, and Get Started a bright star.

Narrator

A screen reader: it reads out loud what the keyboard or mouse is on, windows that open and messages. Turn it on in Settings > Accessibility or with Ctrl + Nebulux + Enter; Ctrl stops the talking. Hold Caps Lock and press W to read the whole window, T for the time, B for the battery or N for the newest notification. Its voice comes with the 4.4 USB.

Copy text from a screenshot

Snipping Tool and Photos have Copy text: they read the words in a picture, right on this computer, and copy them (on the USB).

Screenshots on the clipboard

Every screenshot is also copied, so Ctrl + V pastes it straight into an app or a chat, like Windows.

Night light everywhere

On the USB, Night light now warms the whole display, so YouTube and every other app window gets warmer colors too. On a screen with no backlight, like a desktop monitor, the brightness slider dims every window as well.

Rename this PC

Settings > System > Rename this PC gives your computer its own name, shown in Settings and About.

Screen and sleep

Settings > System > Power: turn the screen off, or put the computer to sleep, after a while with nobody using it. A video or music that is playing keeps it awake.

Volume mixer

Settings > System > Sound has a Volume mixer: each app playing sound gets its own volume and Mute (on the USB).

Processes in Task Manager

Task Manager has a Processes tab: every program on the computer with its processor and memory use, and End task for your own (on the USB).

Remind me

Type remind me to call Sam in 20 minutes, or remind me to stretch at 3pm, in Start or Quick Search and it goes into Reminders, ringing on time. Timers and sums work there too.

A better Paint

Paint has Redo, Ctrl + Z and Ctrl + Y, any color you like, and asks what to call your picture when you save it.

Picture details in Photos

The i button in Photos shows a picture's name, date, size, dimensions and folder, with a way to open the folder.

Graph in Calculator

Calculator has a Graph mode: type up to three functions like sin(x) or 2x+1 and see them drawn. Drag to move around, and zoom with the wheel. Standard mode has memory buttons too: MC, MR, M+, M- and MS.

Timers that keep going

Clock can run several timers at once, each with a name and any length, and they ring even with Clock closed. Type timer 10 min in Start or Quick Search to start one; running timers count down in Widgets.

Week view in Calendar

Calendar has a Week view: your events hour by hour, a line for right now, and click an hour to add an event then.

PDF Viewer

PDF files open in their own window, with zoom, search, rotate and print.

File Explorer with the keyboard

The arrow keys move between files, Shift + arrows select a range, Home and End jump to the first and last, and typing a name jumps to it.

Rename many files at once

Select several files in File Explorer and press F2 or Rename: they get one name, numbered (1), (2), (3), each keeping its file type.

Open with

Right-click a file in File Explorer and pick Open with to choose the app: edit a picture in Paint, or read any file in Notepad.

Color Picker

A new app: pick any color on the screen with Nebulux + Shift + C and copy it as HEX, RGB or HSL. Your recent colors stay in the app.

Text replacements

Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Keyboard: type a short code like omw, then a space, and it becomes the whole phrase (On my way!). Add your own.

Symbols

Nebulux + . opens Symbols: ©, °, ™, currency, math, arrows, Greek and accented letters, typed right where you are typing.

Fold the calendar

The arrow next to the date in the calendar flyout folds the month away, so more notifications fit.

Search finds the setting

Click Start and just type to search. The arrow keys move between the apps in Start, the tiles in Quick Settings, the icons on the desktop, the pages in Settings and the tabs in apps.

Nebulux + M minimizes every window and Nebulux + Shift + M brings them back. Nebulux + Home keeps only the window you are in.

The taskbar from the keyboard

Every switch works with the keyboard

On-screen keyboard

Filter Keys

Settings > Accessibility > Filter Keys ignores a key pressed twice by accident: the same key within half a second counts once.

Toggle Keys

Settings > Accessibility > Toggle Keys plays a high sound when Caps Lock, Num Lock or Scroll Lock turns on, and a low one when it turns off.

Sticky Keys

Press Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Nebulux, then the next key, instead of holding them together. Turn them on in Settings > Accessibility, or press Shift five times.

Bold focus outline

Find counts matches

Read aloud in Notepad

Notepad reads the words you select, or the whole note, out loud. Press it again to stop.

Captions in Media Player

A .srt or .vtt subtitle file with the same name as a video shows as captions; the CC button turns them on or off, and Settings > Accessibility > Caption style makes them bigger or easier to read.

Mono audio

Settings > Accessibility > Mono audio mixes the left and right sound together for every app, so you hear everything in either ear (on the USB).

Messages that wait for you

Settings > Accessibility > Show messages for keeps messages up for up to a minute, and a message never goes while the pointer is on it.

Flash the screen for alerts

Settings > Accessibility > Hearing flashes the screen when an alarm, timer or alert sound plays.

Contrast themes

Settings > Accessibility > Contrast themes: Aquatic, Desert, Dusk and Night sky, strong solid colors that make everything easier to see, like Windows. Left Alt + Left Shift + Print Screen turns one on and off.

Color filters for color blindness

Settings > Accessibility > Color filters can shift the screen's colors for red-green (deuteranopia or protanopia) or blue-yellow (tritanopia) color blindness. Ctrl + Nebulux + C turns them on and off.

Easier reading

Settings > Accessibility > Easier reading puts more space between letters, words and lines in apps, which helps with dyslexia.

Hover Text

Find my pointer

The next window takes over

Programmer calculator

Calculator has a Programmer mode: type a number in hexadecimal, decimal, octal or binary and see the other three, and shift or flip its bits.

Text size
