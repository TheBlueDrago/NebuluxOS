Take a look

Everything where you expect it

Start, the taskbar, Settings and your files are right where your hands go. Then Nebulux OS adds the nebula glow, its own apps and a few things that just work better.

Start and search

Find anything. Pin it your way.

Press the Nebulux key and start typing. Apps, Settings pages and your files show up together, with the details on the right.

  • Pin to taskbar, show on or hide from the desktop, or uninstall, right from search

Settings

Settings that make sense

System, Bluetooth and devices, Network, Personalization, Apps, Accounts, Time and language, Accessibility, Privacy and Nebulux Update, in plain words.

  • Real Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, with clear reasons when a chip can't work
  • Mouse speed from Slower to Faster
  • Brightness, night light, sound output and screens
Battery

A battery icon that tells the truth

The bar inside the battery goes down as it drains. Green while charging, orange at 20%, red at 10%.

  • Battery saver turns the bar yellow and switches off effects to make the battery last
  • It turns on by itself at 20%, and stays off if you turn it off
  • A heads-up at 20% and at 10%

Questions

Good to know

Will it erase my computer?

No. Nebulux OS runs from the USB and never writes to your computer's own drive. Shut down, take the USB out, and your computer starts the way it always did.

Does it cost anything?

No. Nebulux OS is free to download and use, with no ads and no account needed.

Will it run on my computer?

Most PCs and laptops from about 2010 on work: 64-bit, 4 GB of memory or more is best. Macs with Apple chips (M1 and newer) can't start from a USB like this.

Do my files and settings stay?

Yes, when you add a persistent partition while writing the USB with Rufus. Without one, Nebulux OS starts fresh each time and offers a quick Guest start.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Try it in your browser: it's the same Nebulux OS, in a browser tab. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and real apps work only from the USB.
