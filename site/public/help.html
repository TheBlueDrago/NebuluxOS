Help and answers

The quick answers first. The full install guide is on the Download page.

Getting started

Will Nebulux OS erase or change my computer?

No. It runs entirely from the USB stick and never writes to the computer's own drive. Shut down, take the USB out, and the computer starts the way it always did.

Which USB stick should I use?

Any USB stick of 4 GB or more. To keep your accounts, settings and files between restarts, use 16 GB or more and give it a persistent partition in Rufus. USB 3 sticks start faster.

Which computers work?

Most 64-bit PCs and laptops from about 2010 on, with UEFI or the older BIOS. 4 GB of memory works; 8 GB is smoother. Macs with Intel chips can try it (hold Option at power-on); Macs with Apple chips can't.

The computer went straight to its usual system

The boot menu key has to be tapped right as the computer turns on. Try again and tap it several times, or use the Shift and Restart way described on the Download page.

"Secure Boot violation" or a red warning

Turn Secure Boot off in the computer's setup screen (usually F2, Del or F10 at power-on, under Boot or Security). You can turn it back on later.

Using Nebulux OS

My settings are gone every time I restart

The USB was written without a persistent partition, so nothing is saved. Write it again with Rufus and set Persistent partition size to 4 GB or more. Until then, Nebulux OS offers a quick Guest start.

Wi-Fi doesn't show any networks

Open Settings > Network & internet. If the computer's Wi-Fi switch or key turned Wi-Fi off, Nebulux OS says so and turns it back on. A few Wi-Fi chips have no free driver: Settings names the chip. A network cable always works.

A Bluetooth device won't pair

Put the device in pairing mode (usually hold its button until a light blinks), then press Add device in Settings > Bluetooth & devices. Remove it and add it again if it paired before.

How do I install apps?

Open the Nebulux Store, pick an app and press Get. Real apps like Steam, Spotify or Minecraft need internet and a USB that saves changes. Web apps open in their own windows.

How do I pin an app to the taskbar?

Press the Nebulux key (or click the Search box), type the app's name, and press Pin to taskbar in the details on the right. Or right-click the app on the desktop.

What is the Nebulux key?

It's the key between Ctrl and Alt on the left of your keyboard, the one with the logo on it. In Nebulux OS it opens Start, and works with other keys for shortcuts.

The pointer moves too fast or too slow

Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Mouse, then move the slider between Slower and Faster.

Updates

How do updates work?

Nebulux OS checks for an update after you sign in and every few hours. When there's one, it reminds you and asks: Later installs it tonight between 2 and 4 in the morning, Remind me asks again in 30 minutes, and Never skips it.

I pressed Never. How do I update now?

Open Settings > Nebulux Update and press Update now.

Is an update safe?

Every update is checked against its fingerprint before it's used. If an update ever fails to start, Nebulux OS goes back to the version on your USB by itself. Your files and settings are kept.

Do I need to download the new file every time?

No. Only for a brand-new USB. A USB that's already on Nebulux OS updates itself.
